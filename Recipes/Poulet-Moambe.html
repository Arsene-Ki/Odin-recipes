poulet-Moambe dish

Poulet maombe is celebrated as the national dish of both Congo kinshasa and Congo brazzaville , it features tender pieces of chickens simmerd in a rich , velvety, and deepy savory sauce made from the pulp of palm nuts (or palm butter.)

The dish delivers an earthy, distintively central African flavor profile and is typically reserved
