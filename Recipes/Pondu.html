pondu dish

Pondu also known as saka saka, is a beloved greens-based stew made from pounded cassava leaves.It's highlights the absolute staple crop of the region and is slowly simmered with an aromatic blen of vegetables like leeks, onions and bell peppers, all enriched with vibrant red oil.

Often cooked with smoked fish or beef for added depth, Pondu is a thick, highly nutritiouss, and comforting dish that accompanies nearly every celebratory congoleseplatter.
